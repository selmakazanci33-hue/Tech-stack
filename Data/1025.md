As part of our deeper review, we have continued to analyze the Inbound 834s and identified several scenarios where an Inbound 834 is not expected. Before proceeding further, we would like to confirm that these observations align with the intended operational behavior

 

The current population includes 958,218 Enrolled/Pending enrollment enrollee‑pairs. Of these:


843,522 (88.03%) have inbound enrollee evidence
114,696 (11.97%) currently have no inbound enrollee evidence
 

As per Vimo below are scenarios where Inbound 834s are not expected

 

Auto‑renewed records do not generate an Inbound 834 because they are already in CONFIRM status from the prior year.
Enrollments corrected to CONFIRM during Recon do not require an Inbound 834 when InitialPremiumPaid = Y.
CSR‑confirmed enrollments do not trigger an Inbound 834 from the issuer.
Adding a dependent after policy confirmation does not require a new Inbound 834 since the Policy ID and CONFIRM status remain the same.
Rejected 834s do not count as missing evidence, and we are seeking clarity on how issuers track and resend corrected files.
