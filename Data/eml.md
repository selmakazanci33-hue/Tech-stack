No Inbound 834 for Auto Renewed records

As per the updates from Vimo, we do not expect the Inbound 834 for the Auto Renewed records as they are already in CONFIRM status and enrollment was in good standing with the issuer in previous year

 

No Inbound 834 as Enrollment was confirmed during Recon

As per update from Vimo and updates in Section 5.3.7 of Exchange-Issuer Reconciliation Guide, there is a process which auto fixes the Pending status and moves it to CONFIRM status through the Recon Process as long as the Issuer records display Y status for “InitialPremiumPaid”.  

 

No Inbound 834 as the Enrollment was confirmed by CSR / Exchange Staff

When a CSR overrides the status and confirms the enrollment with supporting comments and attachments the Inbound 834 is not expected from the issuer

 

No Inbound 834 expected when the Dependent / Additional Enrollee ID added after Policy Confirmation

Our Spot check revealed scenarios where Enrollee was added few months after the Policy was confirmed through Inbound 834. In these cases, the issuer is not expected to send another Inbound 834 for the new enrollee as there is no change in Policy ID and the same CONFIRM status continues with newly added enrollee

 

Rejected/Resubmitted 834s

Our spot checks revealed several scenarios where 834 files were rejected and the account activity was updated with 'Inbound 834 Rejected' comments. However, we are seeking additional clarity from Vimo on how they track rejected 834s and verify that each rejected file is corrected and resent
